About Us

Authentic Thai Dining in Downtown Reno

Thai Corner Cafe offers a refined yet upbeat atmosphere where guests can enjoy delicious Thai cuisine. Its full-service bar and welcoming dining room provide the perfect setting for cocktails, cold beer, wine, and freshly prepared Thai food.
